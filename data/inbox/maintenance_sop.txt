Section 1.0: Scope
This procedure outlines periodic inspection protocols for chemical processing units.

Section 2.1: Visual Inspection
Inspect all pipe flanges and joint gaskets for signs of weeping, localized pitting, or scaling.

Section 3.4: Ultrasonic Thickness Gauging
Take 4-point ultrasonic thickness readings around the circumference of critical pipe elbows.

Section 4.1: Emergency Shutdown Valves
All emergency shutdown valves must be tested every 6 months for wear and seal degradation.
